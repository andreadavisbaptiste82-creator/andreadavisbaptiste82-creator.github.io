Future Ready AI Curriculum

A connected K–12 pathway

Future Ready AI provides a connected K–12 pathway that helps students understand artificial intelligence, use it responsibly, evaluate its outputs, create thoughtfully, and connect emerging technology to real-world learning and careers. Human judgment, student creativity, responsible use, and age-appropriate learning remain central across every grade band.

Curriculum Overview

Andrea introduces the Future Ready AI pathway — AI Discoverers, AI Explorers, and AI Innovators.

Elementary · Grades K–5

AI Discoverers — Elementary

Curiosity, patterns, human-versus-machine thinking, safe technology use, and foundational digital citizenship.

View Scope & Sequence6 units
  • Unit 1 — What Is Artificial Intelligence? Recognize everyday examples of AI and distinguish between human and machine actions.
  • Unit 2 — Humans, Machines & Patterns. Explore sorting, classification, patterns, and how machines use examples.
  • Unit 3 — Asking Good Questions. Practice clear questions and instructions while noticing how wording changes results.
  • Unit 4 — Creativity With AI. Use age-appropriate tools to support stories, ideas, art, and design while preserving student authorship.
  • Unit 5 — AI Images & Media. Notice clues, ask who created content, and practice simple verification habits.
  • Unit 6 — Safe & Responsible Technology Use. Protect privacy, follow classroom expectations, and ask an adult for help.
Middle School · Grades 6–8

AI Explorers — Middle School

How AI works, prompting, media literacy, bias, privacy, collaboration, and responsible creation.

View Scope & Sequence6 units
  • Unit 1 — How AI Systems Work. Examine data, patterns, predictions, training examples, and common limitations.
  • Unit 2 — Prompting & Communication. Write, test, compare, and revise prompts for clarity, context, and purpose.
  • Unit 3 — Media Literacy & Verification. Evaluate claims, compare sources, and verify AI-generated text and images.
  • Unit 4 — Bias & Representation. Identify missing perspectives, stereotypes, and unfair outcomes in examples and outputs.
  • Unit 5 — Privacy & Digital Footprints. Examine data collection, consent, personal information, and responsible sharing.
  • Unit 6 — Collaborative AI Projects. Apply AI literacy to a team challenge while documenting responsible-use practices.
High School · Grades 9–12

AI Innovators — High School

Applied AI literacy, ethics, career exploration, innovation, and real-world problem-solving.

View Scope & Sequence6 units
  • Unit 1 — Applied AI Literacy. Analyze AI systems, intended uses, limitations, and impacts across school, society, and work.
  • Unit 2 — Prompt Engineering & Evaluation. Design structured prompts, compare outputs, establish criteria, and document iteration.
  • Unit 3 — Ethics & Governance. Evaluate accountability, transparency, privacy, safety, and human oversight.
  • Unit 4 — Bias, Data & Decision-Making. Investigate how data and design decisions may shape outcomes and affect communities.
  • Unit 5 — Career & Entrepreneurship Connections. Explore AI-enabled roles, emerging industries, and responsible business use.
  • Unit 6 — Capstone Innovation Project. Design and present a responsible AI-supported solution to an authentic problem.

The Six Future Ready AI Curriculum Pillars

What AI literacy includes

AI Foundations

Understanding what AI is, how it works, where it appears, and what its limitations are.

Creativity & Innovation

Using AI thoughtfully to support inquiry, design, communication, and problem-solving.

Responsible & Ethical AI

Examining privacy, bias, fairness, transparency, accountability, and human judgment.

Prompting & Communication

Learning how questions, instructions, context, and revision influence AI outputs.

Critical Thinking & Media Literacy

Evaluating accuracy, recognizing manipulation, and identifying missing perspectives.

Digital Citizenship & Future Readiness

Building responsible technology habits while exploring careers and emerging opportunities.
