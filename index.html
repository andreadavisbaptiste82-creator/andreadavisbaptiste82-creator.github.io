The Business Challenge

Many AI training programs are either overly technical or disconnected from daily work.

As organizations accelerate AI adoption, employees need practical learning experiences that build understanding, confidence, and responsible use. The opportunity was to create an approachable learning experience that introduced AI's value without removing the human element from workplace development.

Increase Accessibility

Translate AI concepts into clear, practical workplace language.

Support Adoption

Introduce AI as a tool for productivity, learning, and decision support.

Maintain Human-Centered Design

Reinforce that AI should augment human capability rather than replace human judgment.

The Learning Solution

Grace: a reusable, avatar-led microlearning system.

I designed Grace, a branded AI learning-guide prototype that introduces employees to AI-enabled workplace learning — combining instructional scripting, avatar-led delivery, visual branding, and microlearning into a modular experience for onboarding, leadership development, and just-in-time performance support.

Project Case Study

Business Need

Organizations need practical guidance that helps employees and leaders use AI responsibly without creating unnecessary fear or technical complexity.

Learning Objective

Help workplace leaders apply three responsible-AI checks before using AI-generated information in professional decisions.

Three Responsible-AI Checks

  • 1 Protect the Information
  • 2 Verify the Output
  • 3 Consider the Impact

Workplace Scenario

A manager uses AI to summarize employee-performance notes and recommend development candidates. The manager must assess data privacy, accuracy, bias, fairness, transparency, and organizational criteria before making a decision.

“Responsible AI supports human decision-making — it does not replace accountability.”

My Leadership Contribution

I led the concept development, learning strategy, instructional scripting, avatar direction, visual identity, and prototype design — and defined how the solution could scale from a short microlearning experience into a broader AI adoption and performance-support campaign.

Reflection & Next Steps

Executive Reflection

This project reinforced the importance of combining AI fluency with instructional judgment. The value of the technology is not simply its ability to produce content quickly — it lies in how learning leaders use it to create relevant, ethical, scalable, and performance-focused experiences.

A future iteration would further refine the narrator's vocal consistency and brand alignment through a custom voice model, while preserving the instructional structure, responsible-AI content, visual design, and workplace scenario.

Next Phase

The next phase would pilot the microlearning experience with a representative learner group, validate accessibility and usability, add knowledge checks, and measure changes in confidence, responsible-AI awareness, and behavioral intent.

  • Custom voice-model alignment
  • Interactive knowledge checks
  • Learner branching
  • Accessibility validation
  • Feedback collection
  • Analytics
  • Additional responsible-AI modules
