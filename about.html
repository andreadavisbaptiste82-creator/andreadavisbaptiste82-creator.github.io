About Andrea

One connected career story

Andrea builds human capability at the intersection of learning, leadership, technology, and responsible AI—from enterprise workforce transformation to future-ready K–12 education.

Andrea's path began in the K–12 classroom — teaching computer science, coordinating school technology, and coaching educators through change.

That direct, human-centered instructional experience became the foundation for an enterprise career: leading learning, technology adoption, and change enablement across one of the nation's largest public-sector school systems.

Today, that same throughline connects two parallel commitments — helping organizations build workforce capability for AI and technology change, and helping schools build responsible AI literacy for the students who will inherit it.

Leadership Philosophy

Every learning initiative should begin with a business challenge — not with designing training.

  • Improve performance
  • Increase technology adoption
  • Develop leadership capability
  • Prepare people for change
  • Accelerate transformation

Learning becomes the strategy — not the product. That belief holds whether the learner is a corporate employee or a third-grade student meeting AI for the first time.
