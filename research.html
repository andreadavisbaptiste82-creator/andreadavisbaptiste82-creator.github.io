Research & Dissertation

Dissertation Overview

Andrea is pursuing a PhD in Leadership, ABD (All But Dissertation) — expected December 2027, at Concordia University Chicago. Her doctoral research strengthens her approach to AI-enabled learning, responsible innovation, leadership, and organizational transformation by connecting research with practical workplace application.

Research Problem & Purpose

Ethical equity in AI-driven personalization within workplace learning

As organizations increasingly personalize learning through AI, questions of fairness, transparency, and trust become central to whether that personalization genuinely serves employees or inadvertently disadvantages them. Andrea's doctoral research examines how ethical equity can be designed into AI-driven personalization within workplace learning — and what that means for the people the technology is meant to serve.

Ethical Equity in AI-Enabled Learning

Examining how AI-driven personalization can either advance or undermine fairness, access, and trust across diverse employee populations.

Responsible AI & Governance

Exploring the governance structures, oversight, and accountability practices organizations need as they adopt AI-enabled learning tools.

Human-Centered Adoption

Studying how accessibility, employee trust, and adult-learning principles shape whether AI-enabled learning is genuinely adopted — not just deployed.

Conceptual Frameworks

The lenses informing this work

Responsible AI

Privacy, accuracy, bias, fairness, and transparency as design commitments, not afterthoughts.

Human-Centered Design

Technology in service of human judgment and capability — not a replacement for it.

Adult Learning

Andragogical principles applied to how working adults build trust in and adopt new technology.

AI Governance

Organizational structures for oversight, accountability, and responsible-use standards.

Organizational Adoption

Change-management and diffusion-of-innovation thinking applied to AI-enabled learning tools.

Accessibility & Employee Trust

Equity of access and the conditions under which employees trust AI-supported decisions.

Research-to-Practice Applications

From doctoral inquiry to workplace strategy

Andrea applies this research to help organizations design AI-enabled learning experiences that balance innovation, employee trust, accessibility, responsible use, workforce readiness, and measurable organizational performance — including the frameworks, playbooks, and prototypes featured on the Corporate page.

“Research strengthens the strategy. Practice proves its value.”
